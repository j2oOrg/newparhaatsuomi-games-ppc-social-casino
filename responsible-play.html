A practical way to play

  1. Choose a finish time. Decide how long the session will last before it begins.
  2. Do not chase a result. Random outcomes do not “owe” a player a correction after a losing streak.
  3. Notice the mood. Stop if the game creates frustration, urgency, or a need to continue.
  4. Take a real break. Stand up, move, and switch contexts between sessions.
  5. Keep under-18s out. Do not share access with minors.

The playable areas are intended only for adults aged 18 or over. Device-level parental controls, separate user accounts, and supervised browsing can help prevent access by minors. If a minor has accessed a game, close it and clear the stored age confirmation in the browser’s site data.
